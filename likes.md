## I add to this list when I remember...

## Snoopy 
I am not a collector of any kind, but every time I see Snoopy on anything (clothes, mugs, furniture, towels), I have an urge to buy it and fawn over how cute it is.

## Cycle classes
I always thought I would feel so cringe doing a spin class. From what I've seen, the instructors are always too cheerful, hype you up constantly, and the music is way too loud. Well, I went twice, and both times I was so motivated that I started crying in class. There is something nice about good old cliche movie-like hyping.

## Uptown Girls (2003)
This movie made me cry every time I watched it. 

## Rachmaninoff Piano Concerto No. 2 in C 
I've listened to this concerto more times than I can count and I have yet to listen to it in-person.

## Mouling Rouge (2001)
I am not a big fan of musicals, but this one is perfect. I watched the Broadway musical but nothing beats the movie cast for me.

## Yoga
I started doing yoga frequently in June 2025 and it helped fix my back so much. Now I'm trying to incorporate meditation into the workout itself to engage both mind and body.

## Sentimental Value (2025)

Probably my favorite movie of 2025. I love the subtle acting, unspoken family dynamics, and tapping into feelings that we can't verbally express but can clearly experience when observing someone else go through them.

## Black turtlenecks

I wear them out until they rip and then buy a new one. Goes with everything, instantly elevates an outfit and makes it look higher effort than it is.
